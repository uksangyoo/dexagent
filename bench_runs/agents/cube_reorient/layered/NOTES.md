# cube_reorient at level layered


## Plan / skills
- Strategy: twist gait (two fingers squeeze opposite side faces, orbit about cube's vertical axis, release, regrasp) with yaw feedback; fallback single-finger corner pushes.
- Layers: fingertips (`move_tips`, straight lines) for all finger motion; `reach()` (fingertip IK residual) for runtime planning. No joints/synergy/contacts primitives so far.
- Skills in policy.py: `Ctx.run` (wraps any motion generator, tracks unwrapped yaw like check.py + trace), `Ctx.reach` (cached IK residual), `probe` (reachability maps per finger at 3 heights, logged), `plan_twist` (searches finger pair/faces/height/direction for the largest feasible orbit angle), `twist` (approach-above, descend, close until contact force, orbit, release, lift), `plan_push`/`push` (corner push fallback).

## Iterations
- iter 1 (0/3): probe + twist planner + push fallback. Twist planner found NO pair (pre-grasp standoff/above points 15 mm out were out of reach, and half-edge estimate was 2.5 mm too big: palm face sits at x~24 mm, not 21, in the measured frame). Fell back to thumb corner pushes: ~10-13 deg per push at nominal (reached 36 deg) but drift/tipping at sev 0.5/1 (cube shoved to palm z=7 mm, thumb left pressing 14 N).
  Reach maps (fingertip IK, palm frame): index/middle/ring reach only z~30-80 mm over the palm (index y 0..40, middle y -30..30, ring y -30..10); little only z>=100; thumb reaches nearly everywhere except a hole at y -20..50, z<=50 (x>=45). So pinch pairs: index (+y face, y~+28) vs thumb (-y face). Closeup camera shows nothing useful; use palm/overview.
- iter 2 (0/3): fixed half-edge (a = x_centre - 23.8 mm) and approach (8 mm side standoff, from above if reachable) -> planner now finds thumb/index pairs, but they are kinematic-only: it put the index on the WRIST-side face (index curls over the cube and crushes it into the palm, 27-38 N) and the thumb never registered contact (force 0) on far faces. Squeeze loop ran to its 7.5 mm cap. One sev-1 case reached yaw -97 (in tolerance) but fingers still pressed 22 N at the end. Lesson: IK reach != physically usable contact; fingers 1-4 come from the distal side and can only press the distal face (+z) or the top; the thumb can only press the +y face (and only at z >~ 75 mm when x ~ 45). Also: yaw can jump ~30 deg in the first step (scene start), the checker sees it too, so track yaw from t=0 (done).
- (restart 2026-10-22) An earlier iter_3 folder held only policy.py (killed mid-run, not counted); it is overwritten by the run below.
- iter 3 (pending): new strategy "pin-and-push": a finger (middle/ring/index, chosen by reach) presses the cube's TOP face at its centre (pivot: adds friction against translation, little against rotation), and a pusher (usually thumb on the +y-ish face, offset 7-12 mm from face centre) orbits rigidly about the pin centre in 5 deg / 0.1 s steps, then backs off, lifts and re-plans; yaw feedback with adaptive gain. Added this restart: orbit aborts if pusher force > 6 N or flatness < 0.975; tilt recovery (clear all fingers, wait, re-pin); final clear_all + open fallback if fingers still touch. Layers: fingertips only (move_tips + reach); contacts primitives not used because they push pads along the line to the object origin (wrong for top-face pin / off-centre side push).
  Skills: `clear_all(ctx)` moves every pad near the cube outward + up; `push_cycle` now returns early on force/tilt. A mock api (scratch/mock.py, no physics) dry-runs the policy for Python errors before spending an iteration.
- NOTE on numbering: iterate.py skipped the stale iter_3 folder, so the pin-and-push run above was saved as iter_4 (3rd counted iteration). iter_3/ holds only an unrun policy.py.
- iter 4 (0/3; yaw -3.6 / -2.1 / -37.6): pin-and-push works mechanically but the pin was far too stiff: "+1.5 mm past first contact" gave 2-6 N pin forces (finger stiffness ~1.2 N/mm at the pad), so palm friction torque (~0.9*N*15 mm) exceeded what the thumb can push (it hit the 6 N abort; thumb saturates near 0.65 N m / ~0.1 m). Got only 3-14 deg per 40 deg commanded orbit; re-pinning a drifted cube with 5 N shoved it 15-25 mm toward the wrist, after which only useless index pushes were planned. When the pin missed (0 N) the thumb still got 5-10 deg per push. Fingers other than the thumb never turned the cube as pushers. Released cleanly at the end (finger force ~0.04 N) in all cases.
- iter 5 (final, pending): pin is force-controlled to ~0.3 N (0.5 mm steps) and kept in a 0.08-1.0 N band during the orbit; pin is re-placed at the current cube centre every cycle and lifted BEFORE the thumb backs off (no drag-back); pusher restricted to the thumb when it has a >=10 deg plan; FMAX 4 N; up to 20 cycles; push amount capped at 2.5*|remaining|+5 deg to avoid overshoot.
- iter 5 (0/3; yaw -58.3 / +9.8 (dropped off palm) / +39.3): the light pin (0.3-0.7 N) helped. The first nominal push turned the cube 27.8 deg (the best single push of the study), but each push also shoved the cube 10 mm toward -y/-z (the wrist). After 2-3 pushes the cube was at palm z~40-48, where the thumb's +y-face contacts only stall (11-33 N, 0-1.6 deg). Pins re-placed by ring/middle on a drifted cube sometimes pressed 10-12 N (the force came on before the first 0.5 mm step). At sev 0.5 a late push flipped the cube off the palm. Sev 1 fell back to middle-finger pushes, which do nothing. The unsolved core problem: a one-sided thumb push always translates the cube toward -y/-z, and nothing re-centres it.
- FINAL = 2: no iteration passed a case. iter 2 has the lowest dev yaw errors (54.9 / 23.1 / 7.2 deg; its sev-1 case ended at -97 deg, inside tolerance, but failed only because fingers still pressed 22 N), and it never lost the cube. iters 4/5 released cleanly but turned far less.
