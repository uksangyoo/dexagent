# cube_reorient at level contacts


## iter 1: 0/3
- Policy: runtime planner. Reachability map (api.reach residual) for every finger on a ring of face-centre contact points round the cube (5 deg grid, 2 heights); picks the finger pair / faces / direction with the longest jointly reachable sweep, then approach -> grasp -> api.rotate about palm +x in 10 deg chunks -> release(lift) -> replan.
- Result: planner picked physically absurd pairs (ring crossing to the +y face, index "reaching" the wrist face through the cube): reach() is collision-blind and ignores which side of the cube a finger can come from. Cube got pushed toward the wrist (z 65 -> 2-50 mm), yaw only -5..-35 deg. Also my edge estimate was wrong: cube centre sits 23.5 mm above the palm frame x=0 (nominal 43.5), not 21.
- Learned (nominal reach map, pad 3 mm into face, alpha measured from +z toward +y): thumb reaches alpha 225..355 and 0..80 (not its own +y face!), index 50..200, middle 0..20 & 95..225, ring 135..240 & 310..330, little 235..260 & 25..55. Distal fingers' knuckles sit right over the cube's +z face, so they cannot press it near mid-height. thumb(-y face)+index(+y face) gave a real pinch (7.7 / 6.8 N).
- Skill: `reach_map` (ring of api.reach probes, printed as a digit string per finger) - useful for diagnosis, not as a planner without collision sense. `Track` + `tracked(gen)`: scorer-identical unwrapped yaw updated every control step.
- 'closeup' camera shows nothing useful (hand out of frame).

## iter 2: 0/3
- Changed: fixed edge estimate (half = centre x - 23.5 mm), squeeze 4 mm, fixed side pinch index (+y face) + thumb (opposite face), two grasps, index window alpha 40..140, approach via a point 20 mm above the grasp.
- Result: 0/3. Palm-camera film: to reach the -y face the thumb crosses OVER the cube (its body lies diagonally across the top), pressing it into the palm (palm force 7-13 N, thumb 10-20 N) and shoving it to -y; the little finger also gets involved. Two cases did reach |yaw| ~90 but the cube was knocked off the palm by a regrasp approach with 20+ mm IK residual (thumb), and one clean pinch (index 14 N / thumb 7-10 N) turned the cube +17 deg while my code expected the other sign and stopped as "stalled".
- Learned: thumb is unusable for the -y face (crossing). Distal fingers hang down beside the cube without crossing it: index on the +y side (alpha 60..135), ring on the -y side (alpha 225..335). Need sign-agnostic progress logic and a reach() guard before any approach.
- Camera 'palm' is the useful one (palm-up view, fingers up, +y to the right).

## iter 3: 0/3
- Changed: pinch pair index (+y side) + ring (-y side), both hanging down from the distal end (no crossing); index window alpha 55..150, both turn directions; reach() guard (<5 mm) before grasping; sign-agnostic progress.
- Result: 0/3 (|yaw| 38, 26, 0). The pinch does turn the cube in the commanded sense (pads alpha+ -> yaw+), but only 30-80 % of the commanded angle: fingers press down on the top edges (palm force 8-40 N, finger 15-22 N with 4 mm squeeze) so palm friction resists. The approach via-point 20 mm above was unreachable for the ring (26 mm residual) and the approach/grasp itself kicked the cube 30-40 deg the wrong way. One case never grasped: ring residual > 5 mm at alpha 281 (cube 41.6 mm).
- Learned: ring abduction is the limit (standoff 10 mm on the -y side is out of reach); the hand presses the cube into the palm, so rotate needs the cube unloaded.

## iter_4 folder: killed mid-run by the study pause (does not count; 1 case finished, nominal: yaw +15)
- Policy was iter 3 + reach-checked approach (no via), squeeze 2.5 mm, +3 mm lift translate before rotating. Grasp gave index 14.5 N, ring 17.6 N, palm 29.8 N: the fingers press the cube DOWN (palm force ~ sum of finger forces), the lift did nothing (palm 23 N), and rotate turned the cube only ~10 of 40 commanded deg. Conclusion: friction-driven rotation through a centred pinch loses to palm friction created by the pinch itself.

## iter 5 (4th budgeted): 1/3 (sev 0.5 PASS, yaw -83.6)
- Changed: new mechanism. Antipodal *offset* pinch: +y finger (index/thumb) and -y finger (ring/little) on opposite side faces at points shifted phi (13-36 deg) toward the trailing corners, so api.rotate pushes the faces (normal force x lever) instead of dragging by friction; squeeze only 1.5 mm, pads 2-7 mm above centre. Runtime planner over pair/face/phi/height/sense with api.reach on grasp, approach and the whole sweep; chunked 6-deg rotates with measured progress; release -> open -> replan. Pad targets are given as palm-frame points converted to object-frame points+normals for approach/grasp (my own "fingertip goto" built from the contact primitives).
- Partial result (2 cases in): planner picked index+little, phi 13, every time. The pinch now presses only 3-11 N with palm force 0-2 N (vs 15-30 N before) and the cube follows the pads 1:1 or better (-30 deg for 20 commanded). But release(clear 8 mm, lift 12 mm) with 11 N in the pads flicked the nominal cube off the palm (yaw -69 at release, then gone), and in the sev-1 case the second grasp turned it back the other way (planner switched sense when the locked sense had no plan) -> +4 deg.
- Learned: the little finger reaches the -y face centre (the ring does not); offset pushing beats friction dragging; release must first unload (negative squeeze) slowly before backing off, and the direction switch fallback is harmful.
- Skill: `pads_for` + `goto` (palm-frame pad targets through approach/grasp with explicit normals); `plan` (reach-checked sweep planner).
- Final result 1/3. The PASS (sev 0.5) took 4 grasps: index+little -42, thumb+ring stalled (ring 14 N pressed the cube into the palm, 10-14 N palm force), index+ring with phi 20 turned -49 -> -78 (cube followed even at 16 N because the offset pads push), and the last small grasp kicked it to -90. Scorer yaw -83.6 vs my tracker -90.3: my tracker starts after the 0.5 s settle hold, so ~7 deg of settle/approach rotation is missed. Should start tracking at t=0 and aim for 90 using the scorer's reference.
- Failures: sev 0 = release flick (pads at 11 N backed off 8 mm + lifted 12 mm in 0.6 s threw the cube off the palm); sev 1 = sense switch after a no-plan cycle undid -20 deg. With more budget: unload via squeeze(-2 mm) over 0.5 s before release, never switch sense after progress, start Track at t=0.
