iter 1: LOST. I wrapped the iterate call in `timeout 890`; all 14 GPU slots were busy, it was killed before any case ran (iter_1/ has only policy.py). No results; same policy re-run as iter 2.
Policy idea (v1): thumb-index pinch near the pen end far from the rest (hinge axis = palm z, horizontal, perpendicular to the pen), middle pad on the pen side 21 mm along as a friction clamp for the level hold; lift 200 mm; roll the wrist 90 deg about the pinch axis (pen clamped -> vertical); release the middle (pen now hangs on the 2-point hinge, no torsional friction); roll the wrist back about the hinge so the pen stays vertical while turning 90 deg in the palm frame.
Skill: roll_about(api, xyz0, R0, qp, th0, th1, T) - palm roll about its z through palm point qp, in 9 short carries so the pivot stays fixed in the world.

iter 2: 0/3 (all cases identical). Rest is always at +12 mm along the pen (+y side), so pinch at the -y end (d=33 mm), long side toward palm +y, middle on the 37 mm stub. Middle side-contact read 0 N right after grasping: an unopposed side push just yaws the pen about the pinch (no torsional friction) until the middle unloads, so no friction clamp -> on lift the pen swung down on the thumb-index hinge (hung vertical, nice clean hinge, ~1000 deg/s) and later fell out (stub likely knocked by the middle). Learned: thumb-index pinch at the end = free hinge as designed; the 3rd finger must load the pen along the hinge plane, not sideways.
iter 3 change: middle now presses DOWN on top of the stub (normal force balances the gravity torque, no yaw torque), pinch squeeze 4 mm, middle squeeze 2 mm, middle release retracts toward the palm and +z so the stub can sweep back; added watch() logging.
Skills: pen_state(api) one-line pen tilt / palm-frame axis / finger forces; watch(api, gen, tag, every) logs pen_state while running a motion.

iter 3: 0/3 but progress: middle-on-stub-top clamp gives the level hold in all 3 cases (1.6-2.0 s, tilt 9-16 deg, middle ~0.1 N, pinch ~1.5 N). Failure: as soon as the clamped wrist roll started, pinch forces collapsed (1.5 -> 0.4 -> 0 N in 0.4 s), the pen stayed ~level in the world instead of co-rotating, and dropped. Cause not identified from the logs (camera does not see the top); max pivot 88 deg was only the pen falling.
iter 4 change (last budget): no wrist roll. After the level hold (lift 110 mm), move 45 mm forward to clear the rest, lower the pinch to 25 mm above the table, release the middle so the long end drops ~20 deg onto the table (no big swing), then raise the pinch along an arc centred on the resting tip (radius estimated from the measured pen tilt) so the pen turns ~90 deg in the thumb-index hinge quasi-statically and leaves the table hanging vertical; then lift 90 mm and hold. The table is the "catch" that removes swing energy.
Skill: arc-about-tip lift (hinge point moved on a circle about the estimated contact tip) - quasi-static gravity pivot without oscillation.

iter 4: 0/3, closest yet. Level hold 1.6-1.8 s in all cases. Lowering to the table + middle release + arc-about-tip worked as a slow pivot: the pen turned in the thumb-index hinge from palm +y to ~palm +x (max pivot in palm 90 deg, tilt up to 70 deg from horizontal by arc step 9, no swing). Failure: the pinch force bled away as the pen turned (1.5 N at lift -> 1.0 while lowering -> 0.7 after release -> 0.03 N by mid-arc), and the pen slid out just before vertical and fell. The same force collapse is what killed the iter-3 roll. Likely cause: the pen sits about 16 deg yawed in the pinch (axis_palm z ~ -0.28, from the middle/stub contact), so turning it in the hinge opens the gap between the rounded pads; the squeeze is position-only and nothing re-tightens it.
Next fix (no budget left): squeeze the thumb and index again (api.squeeze) during the arc whenever the pinch force drops below ~0.8 N, and remove the yaw at grasp (middle pad more exactly on top of the stub, or a smaller middle squeeze).
